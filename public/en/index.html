The Northern Shield Project

How does it work? Your path to a safe room, step by step

A free eligibility check is just the beginning. ממ״ד הצפון guides you every step of the way
from initial registration to handover. Every stage is clear, documented, and handled by us.

01

Free eligibility check

Fill in your name, phone number, and locality. Our team will perform an initial eligibility check for state-funded safe room financing, at no cost and with no obligation.

02

Introductory call and inquiry coordination

We will get back to you for an introductory call, understand your property's situation and your needs, and consolidate all initial inquiries with the authorities in one place.

03

Engineering planning and Home Front Command approval

After assessing the property's suitability, we will proceed to a price proposal, engineering design, and obtaining Home Front Command approval before construction begins, within the permit-exempt track and subject to its conditions.

04

Construction and handover

Completion of the safe room through to key handover according to the approved plan, and completion of the inspections, approvals, and reports required at the end of construction.

Under the self-implementation track of the Magen HaTzafon program, the grant for eligible recipients may reach up to 182,000 NIS, subject to eligibility approval, the date of approval, and the program's conditions. The grant is paid according to established milestones. For deadlines and changes to the grant amount, see the question "Until when can an eligibility application be submitted?".

According to the current framework, the possibility of receiving eligibility under the self-implementation track for localities 1–9 km away ends at the end of 2028. For those whose eligibility is approved by 31.12.2027, the grant may reach up to 182,000 NIS; for those whose eligibility is approved during 2028, up to 132,000 NIS. From 2029, the track is closed to new eligibilities. It is therefore advisable to submit the application early: opening an application alone does not guarantee the grant amount. Deadlines are subject to official changes in the program.

A basic safe room can be planned at a net area of 9 sqm, or an expanded safe room at a total net area of up to 15 sqm in accordance with Amendment 163 to the Planning and Building Law: up to 12 sqm for the safe room and up to an additional 3 sqm for a bathroom and/or washroom. Expanding the room beyond 9 sqm within the amendment's addition is contingent on the addition of a bathroom or washroom.

The size and layout are determined according to the property's conditions, applicable regulations, and Home Front Command approval. Execution under the permit-exempt track is contingent on compliance with the track's conditions.

Our payment schedule is aligned with the milestones published by the state for receiving the grant, and subject to the program's conditions and the engagement agreement:

  1. 27% upon receiving the required approvals and excavation of the foundations.
  2. 36.5% upon completion of the safe room's structural frame.
  3. 36.5% upon completion of the safe room.
